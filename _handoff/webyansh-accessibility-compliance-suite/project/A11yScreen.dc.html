✦What this tool does not do

Automated scanning catches about a third of WCAG.

No scanner can confirm that a page is compliant, and any tool that says otherwise is selling you something. Roughly two thirds of the success criteria need a human to judge them: reading order, meaningful alt text, error recovery, and whether a keyboard user can actually finish the task.

We tell you exactly which criteria were tested, which were not, and what a manual audit would add.

Accessibility statement for Cascade Ops

Last reviewed 6 August 2026

Cascade Ops Inc. is committed to making cascadeops.com accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We are working towards conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.2, level AA.

Conformance status

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the standard.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier on this site, email access@cascadeops.com. We aim to respond within five working days.

Assessment approach

Cascade Ops assessed the accessibility of this website by a combination of automated scanning and manual review. Automated testing covers approximately one third of the WCAG success criteria; the remainder were reviewed by hand.
